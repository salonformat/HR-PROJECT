# Illustration prompts

## journey-ink.png

Abstract editorial ink collage about entering a new workplace and slowly finding one's own place. A continuous cobalt-blue brush path passes through five hand-drawn marks: doorway, eye, intersecting chairs, open hand and an off-center starburst. Sophisticated imperfect India ink and dry gouache, torn-paper shapes, transparent background. Cobalt, warm black, acid yellow and coral. No text, people, logos, gradients, vectors or corporate stock style.

## fresh-eyes-ink.png

Abstract visual about fresh perspective and mutual learning: three uneven black ink windows overlap, each revealing a different cobalt-blue mark; a loose coral thread connects them and ends in an unfinished loop, with one acid-yellow paper shape. Sophisticated imperfect India ink and dry gouache, transparent background. No text, people, logos, gradients, vectors or corporate stock style.
